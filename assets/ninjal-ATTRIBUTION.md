## Changes in this adaptation

The official XML archive was converted to JSON. The Hub presents the original Japanese explanations, sense categories, formation groups, example sentences, scene descriptions and notes, and converts supplied reading/emphasis/strikethrough notation into safe display markup. Headings and controls in English are Hub additions, not translations of the Japanese content. No English example translations or AI-generated readings have been added. Source readings have not been independently corrected or verified. NINJAL does not endorse this adaptation.
